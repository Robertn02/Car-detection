      How it works

      1. Find every vehicle

        A detector boxes cars, buses, trucks and motorcycles; a tracker follows each one.

      2. Measure it

        Camera geometry gives each vehicle's distance and motion, in metres checked against the rides' GPS.

      3. Read the road

        Lane paint, signals, signs and plates, from an open street-scene model and text reader.

      4. Decide the relation

        A model trained on labelled vehicles, corrected through the review page.

      5. Add up the exposure

        Per second, per ride and on the map: traffic around the rider, overtakes, close passes, the door zone.
