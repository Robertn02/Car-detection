Reading the overlay

  • Boxes in the colour of the vehicle's relation, labelled with it and how far ahead it is ("same way 9m"); "?" when the model is unsure. Vehicles beyond 25 m get a thin box and no label.
  • Lane lines found in the road paint: white, yellow, and green beside the rider when the rider is in a bike lane.
  • Signals boxed in the lamp's colour ("signal red"); signs with their type or what they say (street names, parking hours, "SPEED LIMIT 35"); plates boxed in yellow with the text read.
  • Top bar: time into the ride, the riding context (most common over 7 s), the rider's speed (from the GPS where the ride has it), the signal ahead and the posted limit.
